São Paulo, July 22, 2026 – For the fifth time, Audi do Brasil, the NGO Litro de Luz Brasil, and the Audi Environmental Foundation have provided selfmade solar lamps to residents of a rural Brazilian region lacking a permanent power supply — this time in the Pantanal region of Mato Grosso do Sul. From June 4 to 10, volunteers and Audi do Brasil employees worked alongside residents to assemble the lights under the guidance of Litro de Luz Brasil. By the end of the campaign, 178 compact lighting units had been handed over, comprising 158 solar lamps and 20 solar-powered streetlights.
