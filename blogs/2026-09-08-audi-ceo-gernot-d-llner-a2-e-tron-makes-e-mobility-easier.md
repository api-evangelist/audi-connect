Ingolstadt/Paris, September 8, 2026 – Audi CEO Gernot Döllner unveiled the Audi A2 e-tron in Paris. With its focus on efficiency, intelligent technology, and everyday usability, the all-electric compact model is a tangible example of Audi’s renewal. The Audi A2 e-tron is specifically tailored to the needs of European customers.
