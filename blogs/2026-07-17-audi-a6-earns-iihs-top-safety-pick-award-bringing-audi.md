The renowned US Insurance Institute for Highway Safety (IIHS) has awarded the Audi A6 its highest safety rating, TOP SAFETY PICK+. With the addition of the A6, Audi now has seven models carrying the organization’s top distinction – more than any other premium brand in 2026. The award-winning models are the Audi A5, A6, Q5 SUV, Q5 Sportback, A6 Sportback e-tron , Q6 SUV e-tron , and Q6 Sportback e-tron .
