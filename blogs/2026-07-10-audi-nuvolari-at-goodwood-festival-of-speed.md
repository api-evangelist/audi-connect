At Goodwood Festival of Speed, the Audi Nuvolari supercar will show what it’s made of on the Hillclimb. The Audi RS 5 is also on display at Goodwood Circuit from July 9 to 12. Shown at speed for the first time, the Auto Union Lucca — a “Rennlimousine,” or racing sedan — adds a touch of history.
