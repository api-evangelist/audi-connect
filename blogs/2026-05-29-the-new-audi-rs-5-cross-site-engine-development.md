The new Audi RS 5 stands for performance at the highest level – and for collaborative development spanning national borders. The close integration between engine development in Neckarsulm and Audi Hungaria in Győr demonstrates how technical excellence, well-defined processes, and mutual trust make high performance possible. The Audi RS 5 will be available in the European market from the end of June – customers can already configure and order their vehicle.
