With new appointments at the international top management level, Audi is systematically advancing knowledge transfer between its subsidiaries as well as between the Production and Technical Development divisions. Effective July 1, 2026, Fermín Soneira will assume the role of Chief Technical Officer at Automobili Lamborghini. He succeeds Rouven Mohr, who recently moved to AUDI AG as Member of the Board for Technical Development on March 1.
